## Liberties kept on purpose

- **One battalion, four towns.** The design (SPEC, 2026-11-08) puts a company from each town in one battalion. Real battalions were raised by region, and on 25 September 1915 the four towns' Loos stories stand on four different stretches of the real front: the Lone Tree (1st Division's front), Loos village (15th), the cemetery and Double Crassier (47th), and the mill's chalk. The battalion is fictional and unnamed, so each town's company fights where its story is.
- **The Lone Tree and the farm company.** The Devons were north of the Lone Tree, not at it; the farm company is not the Devons, so it can be.
- **Fictional people and places.** Blackhaugh, Dunsworthy, the Gresham and Lombard Assurance, the friends, the squire and his son.
- **The "Tower Bridge" towers** are named in the text without a pit number.
