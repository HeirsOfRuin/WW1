# Playtest 2: what to play and what to note

For the author. The game is at https://heirsofruin.github.io/WW1/ (phone or desktop). A whole war is
about 70 screens; most men die before 1918, so a full run is 20 to 40 minutes.

## What changed since playtest 1

- All four towns are written from enlistment to the Armistice: the mill, the pit (two roads,
  with the section or with the tunnellers), the farm and the city.
- Each tour opens on a page of the month and what happened, with the story first; a duty is
  offered on 60% of tours.
- Money sent home shows on the Soldier tab and changes what home looks like at each leave.
- The history was checked and corrected (docs/SOURCES.md).

## Suggested runs (pick two)

1. **The farm or the city to the end.** The newest writing. Volunteer about half the time.
2. **The pit, going to the tunnellers** when offered in early 1916. The other road through the
   same war.
3. **Any town, keeping your head down.** Never volunteer, go to ground in battles. See whether it
   still feels like a story.

## What to note (copy the line, add a word or two)

| Where (town, month) | What you felt or what was wrong |
|---|---|
| e.g. "farm, July 1916" | "the burial scene was too long" / "didn't know what this choice did" |

Things worth a note:
- Anywhere the prose sounded machine-written, or explained what it had already shown.
- Anywhere a choice felt pointless, or you couldn't tell what it would cost.
- Whether the story or the duties felt like the bigger part (the target is about half each).
- Whether the money at leave meant anything to you.
- Whether the war felt too hard, too easy, or about right (target: the Armistice in 5-10% of
  good runs).
- Anything historically off. The checked list is docs/SOURCES.md.

Paste the notes back in any form; they go into docs/FRAME-PLAYTEST2.md the way playtest 1 did.
